It answered anything, even off-topic

The problem

Asked "messi 2007", the assistant wrote a football history. Every answer like that costs money on the AI account and makes a work tool look unprofessional.

What changed

  • Written instructions on every AI request. Madeline helps only with executive-assistant and business work: email, calendar, tasks, clients, SOPs, documents, writing, bookkeeping, research for a work task and translation.
  • A quick off-topic check before every answer. A small, low-cost check reads the question first. If it's off-topic, the person gets a friendly redirect, and the more expensive model is never called.
  • Short follow-ups still work. "Make it shorter", "yes" or "why?" are read with the previous reply, so they aren't mistaken for off-topic questions.

2CriticalLive

It didn't use your business data

The problem

It couldn't answer "What's on my plate today?" with real tasks or meetings. It was given the same 20 tasks for every question, with no due dates, no meetings and no emails.

What changed

  • Madeline looks up what each question needs: tasks, calendar meetings, inbox messages, clients and SOPs, the moment she's asked.
  • She only sees what that person may see. Every lookup runs under the account of the person asking, so the Hub's existing permissions apply. Private emails stay private, and client portal accounts see nothing.
  • "Today" means the person's own today. Times are shown in their timezone, so an EA in Manila at 8am gets today's list, not yesterday's.
  • "My tasks" includes tasks you created and didn't assign. The task form leaves the assignee blank by default, so these were being missed.
  • No more empty "nothing today" answers. If nothing is yours today, Madeline also shows overdue team work, who has it, and what's coming up for you this week.
  • She tells you when your calendar isn't connected, instead of saying you have no meetings, and points you to the Integrations page.
  • Read-only by design. Madeline can find and explain work, but changes are still made in the app.

3HighLive

Five separate AI entry points, now one Madeline

The problem

Users met the AI in five places that each behaved differently, with different names, greetings and histories. Two of them were loaded on every page at once and switched over as the window was resized, losing the conversation.

Before

  1. "Ask AI" button and ⌘K opened an "AI Command Center" pop-up
  2. Madeline side panel with its own history
  3. Floating "Hi! How can I help" bubble with another history
  4. AI Quick Actions page in the sidebar
  5. Unlabelled ✨ icons on meetings

Now

  1. One button. "Ask Madeline" in the top bar opens the same panel on every page.
  2. She knows where you are. With a task, meeting, client or email open, Madeline already has it as context.
  3. One conversation that carries on across pages instead of starting over.
  4. Page buttons feed the panel. "Prep me" on a meeting opens Madeline with the request filled in.
  5. ⌘K is search. Jump to any page or record; the last option is "Ask Madeline about …".

Also in this change

  • Typed commands still work in Madeline's box: "Create a task to…", "Remind me to…" and "Go to calendar".
  • Madeline stays usable while a task or client window is open, which is when she has it as context.
  • On phones she opens as a full-screen panel and closes with a tap outside or Esc.
  • The guided tour now points at "Ask Madeline".
4MediumLive

The wording invited off-topic questions

What changed

  • The text box now reads "Ask about your tasks, clients, meetings…" instead of "Ask Madeline anything".
  • The greeting no longer says "Ask me anything". "Ask AI · Anything at all" went away with the old pop-up.
  • Suggestions come from today's work instead of generic prompts: "Prep for [your next meeting] at 10:00", "2 overdue tasks: what first?", "3 tasks due today", "1 email waiting on a reply". The numbers match the Dashboard.
  • On a quiet day she offers "What's on my plate today?" and "What's coming up this week?". She never says "ask me anything".
5LowLive

Visual bugs in the AI panels

What changed

  • Focus outline: the highlight now follows the rounded input box, instead of a square drawn inside it. The same fix went into the ⌘K search box.
  • Quick actions cut off: suggestion chips now wrap onto a second line, so none are sliced in half.
  • Avatar outside its message: Madeline's small icon now lines up with the top of each reply and holds still, instead of bobbing loose of the bubble.

Other problems found and fixed along the way

  • Messages sent twice. The old "Ask AI" pop-up sent every question to the AI twice, doubling its cost. Madeline sends it once.
  • Silent failures. The floating bubble and the Quick Actions page showed nothing when something went wrong. Errors now appear with a clear message.
  • Meeting brief that never worked. The meeting prep packet's "AI brief" relied on a server feature that was never switched on, so it always fell back to a basic offline summary. It now uses Madeline.
  • A failing check on every update. GitHub's page-publishing check had failed on every update since 20 August. It is fixed and passing.

How to see it

Reload the Hub once (Ctrl + Shift + R) to get the latest version, then:

  1. Click Ask Madeline in the top bar and ask "What's on my plate today?"
  2. Open a task, then ask Madeline "What's the next step?" without naming the task.
  3. On the Dashboard, click Prep me next to a meeting and press Enter.
  4. Press Ctrl + K, type a client's name, and choose "Ask Madeline about …".
  5. Ask something off-topic, such as a sports question, to see the friendly redirect.

How it was checked

  • Each change was built and type-checked, and the Hub's own consistency checks passed.
  • The assistant's server logic was tested against simulated data. That covered timezones (including clock-change days), permissions, overdue work and unassigned tasks, plus attempts to slip instructions in through an email's text.
  • The new panel was clicked through automatically in a browser, on desktop and phone sizes: the button, context, history, "Prep me", ⌘K search, wording and the visual fixes. Every check passed with no errors.
  • Every update was deployed and confirmed live, with all publishing checks passing.

Suggested next steps

  • Calendar "Plan this day" still opens the old Quick Actions screen, because that screen books the time slots it suggests. Moving it into Madeline is a small follow-up.
  • ✨ buttons that write into a text box (Inbox "AI draft", "Write it for me", SOP "Run AI") work as before, but don't use Madeline's work-only rules and wording yet.
  • Connect Google Calendar for each team member on the Integrations page, so Madeline can see everyone's meetings.
  • Turn off GitHub Pages for the repository. The Hub runs on Vercel, and the Pages copy is an old blank page that isn't used.
