Privacy Policy

How MadeEA Hub collects, uses, stores, shares and deletes data, who can see it, and what it never collects.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Who we are and who this covers

MadeEA Hub (https://hub.madeea.io) is the private workspace operated by MadeEA ("we", "us"), an executive assistant agency. It covers two groups of people:

  • Executive assistants and staff who hold accounts. Access is invite-only; there is no public sign-up.
  • Clients of MadeEA, who may hold a client portal account, and whose contact details, notes and correspondence are kept here as records so their assistant can do the work.

Questions about this policy or your data: princeacquahandam@gmail.com.

Google account data

What we access. When you connect your Google account, MadeEA Hub reads your Gmail messages (sender, recipients, subject and body), sends emails you write and send from inside the Hub, reads your Google Calendar events, and adds events you create in the Hub. It also reads your name and email address to identify the connection.

How we use it. Only to show your mail and calendar inside the Hub, sort your mail into Urgent, Reply, Delegate and Archive, send replies you write, and answer the requests you make to Madeline, the Hub's AI assistant, about your own work.

AI processing. To sort mail and answer your requests, the relevant message text is sent to OpenAI's API for processing. Google data is never used to train AI models, ours or anyone else's.

Sharing. We don't sell Google data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the processors named in this policy (Supabase for storage, OpenAI for AI processing) to provide the features above. Nobody at MadeEA reads your mail unless you ask us to, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

Storage and protection. Synced mail and events are stored in our database (Supabase), encrypted in transit and at rest, and are readable only by the account that connected them. Access tokens are stored server-side and never shown in the browser.

Deletion. Disconnect at any time from Integrations in the Hub, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have synced Google data deleted, email princeacquahandam@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days.

MadeEA Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other data we collect

Account details: name, email address, role and workspace.

Work records: tasks, notes, end-of-day reports, client records and files you add.

Other connected accounts: if you connect Outlook, Teams, Slack, Discord, WhatsApp or Instagram, messages from those accounts are synced in the same way. Personal mailboxes and chats (Gmail, Outlook, Teams) are readable only by the account that connected them, with no administrator override. Shared team channels (Slack, Discord, WhatsApp, Instagram) are visible to the workspace.

While an assistant is clocked in: clock-in and clock-out times, and, after the assistant grants the browser's screen-share permission, screenshots of the whole screen roughly every ten minutes, plus counts of keystrokes and mouse events and idle time. The content of what is typed is never recorded; only the number of keypresses. Nothing is recorded outside a shift. Screenshots are visible to the assistant and to reviewers with permission, and are kept for ninety days by default.

Usage analytics

With your permission, the Hub records which pages are opened and which buttons are used, so we can see which features help. It is asked for once and can be turned off at any time in Settings. Names, email addresses, task titles, notes and message text are never recorded, and the Inbox is never recorded. The service is PostHog, hosted in the EU. Events are kept for twelve months and session replays (with all text hidden) for thirty days.

Who we share data with

We use these service providers only to run the Hub: Supabase (database and storage), Vercel (hosting), OpenAI (AI processing), PostHog (analytics, with consent) and Google (when you connect your account). We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

Your choices and rights

You can disconnect any connected account, turn analytics off, and see what has been recorded about you on the Screenshots and Time pages. To ask what we hold about you, or to request correction or deletion, email princeacquahandam@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

Changes

Material changes to what is collected or who can see it will be posted on this page, with a new date at the top.
